# Kriti (कृति) — 4-Minute Video Demonstration Script

**Submission Target**: 3 to 5 minutes demo video on YouTube or Google Drive.  
**Speaker Tone**: Confident, empathetic, and respectful of traditional craft heritage.

---

### Segment 1: The Problem & The Solution (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Visual**: Show Home Page (`/`) with the editorial poster layout, *Rozha One* wordmark, and subtle drifting jaali background.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Namaskaram and welcome to Kriti. Across India, millions of master artisans preserve millennia-old living craft traditions—from Kerala Murals and Madhubani to Warli and Pattachitra. But in today's digital economy, they face a severe barrier: while they are fluent in their regional mother tongues, typing marketing copy in English is daunting. Worse, when their sacred works are circulated online, the cultural context, the natural mineral pigments, and the artist's name are stripped away by commercial middlemen.*
  >
  > *Kriti solves this. It is a lightweight, mobile-first showcase where cultural context travels with every share, built for the ART//CODE hackathon under the theme 'From Craft to Click – Artist Visibility'."*

---

### Segment 2: Artist Studio & Voice-Driven Storytelling (0:45 – 1:30)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/login`, demonstrate passwordless magic link, and open `/dashboard` (Profile & Biography tab). Show the **Voice "Tell Your Story"** mic button.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Let's look at the artisan's journey. Signing in requires no passwords—just a single magic link. In the Profile Editor, the artisan can enter their name in both English and their native script—such as Malayalam or Devanagari.*
  >
  > *Instead of typing a long biography, the artist taps our native Web Speech voice recorder. They select their mother tongue—Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, or English—and speak naturally. In real-time, their spoken words are captured accurately into the biography field without any expensive third-party APIs or generative distortion. We also support direct WhatsApp numbers, location districts, and portrait uploads."*

---

### Segment 3: Artwork Upload with Cultural Context (1:30 – 2:15)
- **Visual**: Switch to the **Artworks Gallery** tab. Click **Add Artwork**. Select an image, watch it compress client-side, and show the **Guided Storytelling Prompts**.
- **Narration**:
  > *"When uploading an artwork, our client-side compressor optimizes high-resolution photos on the device down to under 5MB, ensuring smooth uploads over rural mobile data.*
  >
  > *Notice this crucial section: Cultural Context. We provide three guided prompts: 'What does this artwork depict?', 'Who taught you the technique?', and 'What is the sacred symbolism?'. The artisan speaks their answers, tags their medium and price, and affirms our mandatory ethical declaration: 'This work is mine, or I have permission to share it.' Once saved, it is immediately live."*

---

### Segment 4: The Public Portfolio & Story-Rich Lightbox (2:15 – 2:45)
- **Visual**: Open the public portfolio at `/a/:slug` on mobile view (375px emulation). Scroll the gallery and click an artwork to open the uncropped lightbox.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Every artisan receives a permanent, mobile-friendly portfolio URL, like `/a/narayan-marar-kerala-mural`. On mobile, it displays the artist's portrait in an authentic arch frame, their regional name, and sample profile designation.*
  >
  > *When a patron taps an artwork, it opens in an uncropped lightbox. Crucially, the valuation, medium, and the full cultural narrative are presented prominently. The sacred story is never lost."*

---

### Segment 5: Cultural Context That Travels — Share Cards, Craft Tags & Captions (2:45 – 3:20)
- **Visual**: Click **Share Card**, show the 2x PNG download. Then click **Craft Tag** and **Captions**.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Here is how cultural context travels. Tapping 'Share Card' generates a high-resolution 2x PNG mounted-print card containing the artwork, artist attribution, location, and story snippet—ready for Instagram or WhatsApp.*
  >
  > *For physical exhibitions, the artist can tap 'Craft Tag' to print packaging certificates, or download a high-contrast stall QR code for visiting cards.*
  >
  > *And when posting online, our Multilingual Caption Generator generates pre-written captions in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, or English across four tones—Storyteller, Festive, Collector, and Minimal—with a one-click copy button and a reminder for native-speaker review."*

---

### Segment 6: Direct Patron Enquiries & Studio Inbox (3:20 – 3:45)
- **Visual**: Fill in the **Enquire / Commission** modal as a patron. Then switch back to the artist's dashboard **Enquiries & Commissions** tab to show the message received with WhatsApp/Email reply buttons.
- **Narration**:
  > *"When a collector wishes to buy or commission a work, they submit our enquiry form—protected by an invisible honeypot defense against bot spam.*
  >
  > *The enquiry arrives instantly in the artist's private dashboard. With one tap, the artist can reply directly via WhatsApp or email. There are zero platform cuts, zero intermediary markups, and 100% fair trade."*

---

### Segment 7: Limitations & Closing (3:45 – 4:00)
- **Visual**: Return to the Discovery Home page showing live database counts and search filters.
- **Narration**:
  > *"A quick note on limitations: the Web Speech API works natively on Chromium and Android browsers, with typing fallback on others. We intentionally do not hold payments to avoid taking cuts from artists.*
  >
  > *Kriti proves that cutting-edge web technology can dignify and protect ancestral heritage. Built with React 19, Tailwind v4, and Supabase for the ART//CODE Hackathon. Thank you!"*
